SOURCE NOTES

What this evidence can tell us

01 / Definition

Youth unemployment is the share of the labour force aged 15–24 without work, available for work and seeking employment. The labour force includes employed and unemployed people. Students outside the labour force are excluded.

This age band is not a fixed generation. This first investigation does not measure a youth–adult gap or prove generational disadvantage.

02 / Provenance

World Bank WDI · SL.UEM.1524.ZS ↗, sourced from the ILO modelled estimates database ↗. Annual, both sexes. Estimates harmonise sources and may be revised; they are not all direct survey observations.

03 / Limits & missingness

Every comparison uses the same selected year. Missing observations appear as “No data”; they are never zero, interpolated or replaced with an earlier year. World Bank regional and income aggregates are excluded from economy rows.

The world rate is the published World Bank aggregate, not an average of the visible rows. Low unemployment can coexist with informal, insecure or poorly paid work. These figures do not measure job quality, inactivity or NEET status.
